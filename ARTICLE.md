# Trade Strategy Skill — The AI's Strictest Trading Coach

## When trading with AI, the real barrier isn't the technology — it's the strategy layer.

You've got an AI agent. It can swap tokens, read on-chain data, and execute faster than any human. So you tell it: "make me money trading." And then... nothing useful happens. It buys something random, or asks you a wall of questions you don't have answers to, or just sits there waiting for instructions you don't know how to give.

The AI isn't the problem. The tools exist — Jupiter for swaps, live feeds from Pump.fun, Raydium, Metaplex, Bags, Moonshot. The infrastructure is there. What's missing is the bridge between your intent and the AI's execution.

You know you want to trade. But what market cap should you target? How much per trade? When do you take profit — 2x? 10x? Never? What separates a good entry from a rug pull? These are the questions that matter, and most people don't have structured answers. So the AI has nothing to work with. An agent without a strategy is just a degen with unlimited speed.

Thousands of people want to use AI to trade profitably. They have SOL in their wallet, tokens on their watchlist, and the motivation to act. But they stall — not because they lack capital or tools, but because they can't translate their gut feeling into a clear set of rules their AI can follow.

The technical barrier to trading is gone. The strategy barrier is what's left.

**Trade Strategy Skill removes that barrier entirely.**

## What is Trade Strategy Skill?

Simply put, this is a skill file installed into your AI agent.

Install it into the AI agent you use (OpenClaw, Claude Code, or any agent that supports skill files), and your AI's behavior will change.

When you tell it "I want to trade" or "set up my strategy," it won't start buying random tokens right away.

It will start by talking to you and, through a series of multiple-choice questions, transform the vague trading idea in your mind into a **clear, executable trading plan** — one that the AI follows with discipline, so you don't have to.

The entire process is divided into five stages.

## What are the six stages?

### Stage 1: Platform Selection 🏗️
*"Where do you want to hunt?"*

The AI asks you which Solana token launch platforms to monitor. All multiple choice — no technical knowledge needed.

- Pump.fun (the memecoin factory)
- Raydium LaunchLab (Raydium's native launcher)
- Metaplex (the OG Solana token infrastructure)
- Bags (presale & fair launch platform)
- Moonshot (DEX Screener's launcher)
- All of the above

It also asks which DEX you prefer for executing swaps: Jupiter (aggregator, best prices), Raydium direct, or PumpSwap.

Every question has an option: **"Let AI decide for me."** You can complete your entire trading plan even if you've never traded before.

### Stage 2: Entry Strategy 🎯
*"When should I buy?"*

This is where the plan gets specific. The AI asks:

- **Target market cap for entry?** Micro ($1K–$10K), Early ($10K–$50K), Mid ($50K–$250K), or Established ($250K–$1M). This determines how early — and how risky — your entries are.
- **Buy amount per trade?** From micro bets (0.05 SOL) to larger positions (5 SOL). You set what you're comfortable losing.
- **Max concurrent positions?** 3, 5, 10, or unlimited. This controls your total exposure at any time.

The AI explains each option in plain language. No jargon. No assumptions.

### Stage 3: Safety Filters 🛡️
*"What should I avoid?"*

This is where Trade Strategy Skill protects you from yourself — and from scammers.

- **Minimum liquidity?** Skip tokens with thin liquidity that you can't exit.
- **Dev wallet limit?** If the developer holds more than X% of supply, skip it. Classic rug signal.
- **Minimum holder count?** Avoid tokens held by just 5 wallets pretending to be a "community."
- **Rug pull protection?** Auto-skip tokens where mint authority isn't revoked, freeze authority is enabled, or a single wallet holds over 25%.

Most beginners lose money not because they pick bad tokens — but because they don't filter out the obvious scams. This stage does it for them.

### Stage 4: Take Profit Strategy 📈
*"When do I cash out?"*

The number one mistake in memecoin trading: not having a take-profit plan. You watch a 10x become a -50% because you were waiting for 100x.

The AI offers three styles:

- **Ladder** — Sell in portions at milestones. Example: sell 25% at 2x, 25% at 5x, 25% at 10x, hold 25% as a moon bag.
- **Moon Bag** — Sell most of your position to secure profits, keep a small bag forever in case it truly moons.
- **All-or-Nothing** — Set one target. Hit it or hold. (Degen mode.)

Then it asks about **stop losses**: -30%, -50%, -70%, or no stop loss. The AI explains the trade-off for each.

### Stage 5: Review, Configure & Deploy 🚀
*"Here's your trading plan. How should I run it?"*

The final stage brings everything together. First, the AI asks how you want it to operate:

- **Alert Only** — The AI finds opportunities and notifies you. You decide whether to buy or sell.
- **Semi-Auto** — The AI buys automatically based on your filters, but asks for your approval before selling.
- **Full Auto** — The AI handles everything. You review the daily report over coffee.

It also asks where to send alerts (Telegram, Discord, or in-session) and how often to report (every trade, daily summary, or weekly digest).

Then the AI generates your complete **Trading Plan Summary** — a clean document showing every parameter you selected:

- Platforms monitored
- Entry criteria and buy amounts
- Safety filters active
- Take profit ladder
- Execution mode and alert preferences
- Risk metrics (max capital at risk, estimated trade frequency)

You review it. Adjust if needed. Once you confirm — the AI activates the plan and starts working.

Your trading plan becomes the AI's **constitution**. It will not deviate from it. Every buy, every sell, every skip follows YOUR rules.

## How to use (for beginners)

Don't let "install a skill file" scare you. Many people hesitate as soon as they see technical-sounding instructions. Here's the reality: it's 6 steps, and you'll be trading in under 10 minutes.

### Step 1: Choose an AI agent platform.

If you haven't installed anything, we recommend OpenClaw — it connects directly to Telegram and runs 24/7, even when your computer is off. Any agent platform that supports skill files will work.

### Step 2: Install the skill file.

If you use OpenClaw or Claude Code, open the terminal, copy and paste the following command, and press Enter:

```
curl -L https://raw.githubusercontent.com/castlejnr2-droid/trade-strategy-skill/main/SKILL.md -o SKILL.md
```

This command downloads the skill file to your current directory. Your AI agent will automatically read it on startup, and the skill takes effect immediately.

### Step 3: Just say what you want.

Don't overthink it. Just talk to your AI like you'd talk to a friend:

- "I want to trade memecoins on Pump.fun"
- "Set up a sniper strategy for new token launches on Solana"
- "I have 5 SOL and I want to flip memecoins — help me build a plan"
- "I want to copy whale wallets and auto-trade"

The more specific you are, the better the plan. But even "I want to make money trading" is enough to start — the AI will figure out the rest through questions.

### Step 4: Answer the multiple-choice questions.

The AI will start asking you questions — all multiple choice, all in plain language — for about 6–8 rounds. Every question includes the option **"Let AI decide for me."** If you have absolutely no idea, just select this option for every question, and the AI will make reasonable recommendations based on current market conditions and best practices.

You don't need to know what "mint authority" or "liquidity pools" mean. The AI explains each option before you choose.

### Step 5: Confirm your Trading Plan.

After answering all questions, the AI gives you a complete **Trading Plan Summary**. This includes your selected platforms, entry criteria, safety filters, take-profit ladder, execution mode, and risk metrics.

Read through it. If something doesn't feel right, say "change my take profit to ladder style" or "lower my buy amount." Once you're satisfied, reply **"Confirm"** and the AI activates your plan.

### Step 6: Let it run. Check your reports.

Once your plan is live, you don't need to do anything most of the time. The AI monitors launches, filters tokens through your safety rules, executes trades according to your strategy, and takes profits at your specified levels.

It will notify you based on your alert preferences — every trade, daily summary, or weekly digest. When it faces a real decision that requires your input — like "this token passed all filters but the dev is a known scammer from a watchlist, should I still buy?" — it will stop and ask you.

Apart from that, just let it run.

## Key lessons from real trading

**First, the more specific your strategy, the better the results.** The difference between "I want to trade" and "I want to snipe new Pump.fun launches under $10K market cap with 0.1 SOL, ladder out at 3x and 10x, skip anything where dev holds more than 10%" is enormous. You don't need to be a trading expert, but you need to think about: how much am I willing to risk? How early do I want to enter? When do I want to get out? The more you've thought about it, the fewer questions the AI asks, and the closer the plan matches what you actually want.

**Second, spend more time on the planning stage — it saves everything later.** Many people want to rush through the questions and start trading immediately. This is the easiest trap to fall into. If your filters are too loose, you'll buy into rugs. If your take-profit is wrong, you'll watch gains evaporate. Spend the extra 2 minutes reviewing your plan before confirming. Check: are your safety filters strong enough? Is your position size something you can actually afford to lose? Is your take-profit realistic?

**Third, your Trading Plan is your most important asset.** It's not just a settings file — it's your trading discipline in written form. When the market is pumping and FOMO hits, your AI doesn't feel FOMO. It follows the plan. When everything is crashing and panic sets in, your AI doesn't panic. It follows the plan. The plan is what separates traders who make money from traders who give it all back.

This idea matters more than people realize. The plan isn't just for "right now" — it's for your future self and the AI. If you want to adjust your strategy three months from now, without a saved plan, the AI has to re-learn everything about your preferences from scratch — extremely time-consuming and prone to mistakes. With your Trading Plan saved, the AI can compare your current performance against the plan and make precise adjustments. The difference in efficiency is night and day.

More importantly, the Trading Plan serves as the **"constitution" governing your AI.** A core constraint of this skill's design is that the AI must trade strictly according to your confirmed plan — it cannot arbitrarily change your position sizes, skip your safety filters, or ignore your take-profit levels. This constraint only works because the plan exists as a written document.

**Fourth, don't panic when trades go wrong — let the AI handle it.** Losses are inevitable in trading. This is normal. It's not your fault, and it's not the AI's fault. These are simply inherent aspects of trading volatile markets. The design principle of this skill is: losses must be recorded, analyzed, and learned from — they cannot be hidden or ignored. So when you see the AI say "trade hit stop loss, recording and adjusting" — this is a good thing. It means the system is working exactly as designed, protecting your capital.

You don't need to understand every market movement. Just review your reports. If the AI asks you a specific question — like "your strategy targets Pump.fun launches but 90% of volume has shifted to Raydium LaunchLab this week, should I add it?" — just make the call. Apart from that, let the system run.

**Fifth, start with alert-only mode.** Many beginners are nervous about giving an AI full control over their money — and they should be. That's healthy caution. Start with **Alert Only** mode. Let the AI find opportunities and notify you. YOU press the buy button. YOU decide when to sell. Watch how it performs for a week. Build trust. Then, when you're confident, switch to Semi-Auto or Full Auto. There's no rush.
